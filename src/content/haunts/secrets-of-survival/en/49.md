**Scenario Card:** For Sale
**Haunt Trigger:** Dagger · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*The realtor informs you that your offer was not accepted. "I've come to a quite satisfactory agreement with a new buyer," she says, and for the first time, you notice how sharp her teeth look. "The contract's already been signed, and there's a certain little clause that I'm particularly excited about. Oh, and don't bother trying the doors—they're already locked." She turns to acrid smoke and sinks into the floorboards.*

### Setup
1. Each hero takes a Blood token ![[blood.png|18]] and places it on their character board.
2. The player to the left of the traitor will take the first turn after setup.

---

## Objective
You win when you burn the Contract.
- Steal the Contract from the Demon.
- Burn the Contract.

## Tokens Needed
- ![[blood.png|26]] Blood Tokens
- ![[contract.png|26]] Contract Token
- ![[demon.png|26]] Demon Token

## Rooms with Fire
- Furnace Room – Basement
- Ritual Room – Basement
- Kitchen – Ground Floor
- Laboratory – Ground Floor
- Charred Room – Ground/Upper Floor

## ![[contract.png|30]] The Contract
*An infernal document; the parchment is warm to the touch.*

The Contract can be traded between heroes, using the normal trading rules. The traitor cannot hold or touch the contract.

## When You Attack the Demon with Might
If you win, you may steal the Contract from the Demon (if they have it) instead of dealing damage.

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Burn the Contract**
> While you have the Contract, make a Knowledge ![[knowledge.png|18]] roll. If you are in a **room with fire**, add 4 to the result of your roll.
> - **7+** You burn the contract. **You win!**
> - **4–6** Nothing happens.
> - **0–3** Take 1 Physical damage. *Ouch! It burns!*

## Blood Tokens
*You prefer it inside your body.*
- Blood tokens ![[blood.png|18]] cannot be traded, and a hero may not pick up a Blood token ![[blood.png|18]] that has been dropped.
- If you die or take Physical damage, place your Blood token ![[blood.png|18]] on your tile.
- If you don't have your Blood token ![[blood.png|18]] at the end of your turn, take 1 die of Mental damage.

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Perform Blood Magic**
> If you have your Blood token ![[blood.png|18]], take 1 die of Mental damage and gain 1 Might.

## If You Win
*You're alive, but you also haven't found a house to buy. Obviously, the former is more important—you really do like being not murdered by a demon, but still… you thought this house was the one.*
