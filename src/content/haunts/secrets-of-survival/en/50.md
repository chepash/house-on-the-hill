**Scenario Card:** Cursed!
**Haunt Trigger:** Armor · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*Local rumors tell that this mansion was once overrun with some type of living, growing flesh. You all assumed it was just an urban legend, until you all started having the same, recurring nightmares that led you to the house. You're startled to hear your friend start screaming from somewhere in the house, and when the screams stop abruptly, the hairs on the back of your neck stand up. You're going to have to destroy that thing before it gets you, too.*

### Setup
1. Set aside **(3/4/4/5)** Trap tokens ![[trap.png|18]]. These are Explosives.
2. The player to the left of the traitor will take the first turn after setup.

> [!clarify] Clarification (not from the book)
> **(3/4/4/5)** in round brackets is the usual player-count notation, like {3/4/5/6}: 3 tokens with 3 players, 4 with 4 or 5, and 5 with 6.

---

## Objective
You win when you destroy the house.
- Set Explosives to rig the house to explode.

## Tokens Needed
- Number Track
- ![[construct.png|26]] Construct Token
- ![[trap.png|26]] Trap Tokens – Explosives

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Set Makeshift Explosive**
> While on any tile, bury an Item or Omen. Then, make a Speed ![[speed.png|18]] or Knowledge ![[knowledge.png|18]] roll.
> - **6+** Place an Explosive on your tile. If there are no Explosives left, you may move an already-placed Explosive to your tile. *Unconventional, but just crazy enough to work.*
> - **0–5** Nothing happens. Do not place an Explosive. *You destroy the trap before it is set.*

## Consumed Rooms
The Construct is Consuming rooms. Consumed room tiles are turned face-down. They are considered to have no symbols or text, and they have doorways on all 4 sides. Landing tiles cannot be Consumed. Explosives remain on Consumed rooms when they are turned over. You may place Explosives on Consumed rooms.

> [!clarify] Clarification (not from the book)
> Landing tiles are all three starting tiles: the Basement Landing, the Upper Landing and the whole large ground floor tile (Ground Floor Staircase, Hallway and Entrance Hall). None of them can be Consumed.

## At the End of Your Turn
If all of the Explosives have been placed, and there is at least one Explosive in each region, **you win!**

## If You Win
*You look back as the house collapses, odd explosions rocking its structure. Not all of you made it out, but at least that thing is gone for good. You hope.*
