**Scenario Card:** Cursed!
**Haunt Trigger:** Dagger · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*You hear high-pitched laughter coming from all sides of the house. Your phones begin to buzz—strange, because you've been searching for a signal all evening. One of your companions smiles, their lips stretching so far that the corners of their mouth almost reach their ears. In one hand is their phone. In the other, a dagger. They sneer at you, "Forward this or die."*

### Setup
1. The heroes have no additional setup steps.
2. The player to the left of the traitor will take the first turn after setup.

---

## Objective
You win when you kill the traitor and all of the Gremlins in the house.

## Tokens Needed
- ![[small-monster.png|26]] 3 Small Monster Tokens – Gremlins
- ![[rune-1.png|26]] 3 Rune Tokens

## Tiles Where You Can Get a Signal
- Tower – Upper Floor
- Gallery – Upper Floor
- Observatory – Upper Floor

## Gremlin Magic
- You cannot attack a Gremlin that doesn't have a Rune token ![[rune-1.png|18]] on it.
- If you attack a Gremlin that does have a Rune token ![[rune-1.png|18]] on it, it is killed instead of stunned.

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Do Research on your Phone**
> While on a tile where you can get a signal, make a Knowledge ![[knowledge.png|18]] roll. *There has to be more information on these gremlins….*
> - **5+** Place a Rune token ![[rune-1.png|18]] onto a Gremlin. You can now attack this Gremlin. On a successful attack, it will be killed instead of stunned.
> - **0–4** Take 1 Mental damage.

## At the End of Your Turn
Draw a message out of the hat and show it to the traitor. *You receive an email.*

> [!clarify] Clarification (not from the book)
> The "hat" is the top of the game box, where the traitor put the folded messages during setup (the traitor's setup is read aloud).

## If You Win
*You discover that your friend's murderous rage came from a cursed email they had received on their phone. After some digging, you learn that it came from those gremlins—somehow, those ancient pests learned how to use the internet. Although you put an end to the gremlins' antics, your victory came at the cost of your friend's life. As you close their lifeless eyes, you shudder at the too-wide grin still frozen on their face. You startle as their phone buzzes with a notification: "FWD: OPEN THIS!! URGENT!!"*
