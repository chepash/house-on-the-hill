**Scenario Card:** For Sale
**Haunt Trigger:** Idol · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*You split up to find the realtor, who for some reason wasn't waiting out front. Just as you think to yourself, "Remodeling this place will cost a fortune," the lights flicker, and you hear an odd mechanical hum. You get the strange feeling that the house is shivering in anticipation. Suddenly, a radio blares to life beside you. It's probably just bad wiring, but why is it calling your name?*

### Setup
1. Set the Number Track to 0.
2. Place 5 Searched tokens ![[searched.png|18]] near the Number Track.
3. The player to the left of the traitor will take the first turn after setup.

---

## Objective
You win when you overload all of the Generators.
- **Sabotage Electronics** and **Disable Traps** to find supplies to help you **Overload the Generators**.

## Tokens Needed
- Number Track
- ![[on.png|26]] 5 ON/OFF Tokens – Generators
- ![[trap.png|26]] 5 Trap Tokens
- ![[searched.png|26]] 5 Searched Tokens – Sabotage

## Tiles with Electronics
- Panic Room – Basement
- Vault – Basement
- Kitchen – Ground Floor
- Laboratory – Ground Floor
- Game Room – Basement/Upper Floor

## Triggering a Trap
When you enter a tile with a Trap token ![[trap.png|18]], take two dice of General damage. The Trap stays on the tile.

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Disable a Trap**
> While on or adjacent to a tile with a Trap token ![[trap.png|18]], make a Speed ![[speed.png|18]] roll. If you are on the same tile as the Trap, add 2 to the result of your roll.
> - **6+** Remove the Trap and give it to the traitor. Move the Number Track up by 1.
> - **0–5** Nothing happens.

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Sabotage Electronics**
> While on a **tile with electronics**, make a Knowledge ![[knowledge.png|18]] or Might ![[might.png|18]] roll.
> - **5+** Move the Number Track up by one. Place a Searched token ![[searched.png|18]] on the tile.
> - **0–4** Nothing happens. ***ZZZZAAP***
>
> You cannot Sabotage Electronics on a tile that already has been sabotaged.

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Overload the Generator**
> While on any tile with a Generator, roll dice equal to the number on the Number Track.
> - **5+** Flip the Generator token to OFF. If all Generators are OFF, **you win!**
> - **0–4** End your turn.

## If You Win
*You don't know what was living in the wires here, but it felt enormous. You take your escape as victory enough and hope that whatever it is never finds its way out of this house.*
