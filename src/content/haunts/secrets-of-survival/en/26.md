**Scenario Card:** A Mysterious Invitation
**Haunt Trigger:** Holy Symbol · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*The evening was fun, until one of your friends disappeared. As you searched for them, you were struck by how much your friend resembles the people in the portraits that adorn every wall of the house. You begin to hear rhythmic chanting echoing through the halls, "Hail The Elder. Now comes our master from beyond," and every window and outside door abruptly slams shut. There's something sinister going on here, and you'll need to stop it if you're to have any hope of getting out alive.*

### Setup
1. Place the Altar token ![[altar.png|18]] on any Ground Floor tile.
2. If the Ritual Room has not been discovered, search the tile stack for it and place it in the Basement, using normal tile-placement rules.
3. The player to the left of the traitor will take the first turn after setup.

---

## Objective
You win when the traitor is dead, the Tribute has been ruined, and the Altar destroyed.
- You cannot destroy the Altar until you have Ruined the Tribute and killed the traitor.

## Tokens Needed
- ![[altar.png|26]] Altar Token
- ![[small-monster.png|26]] Small Monster Tokens – Relatives
- ![[dark-queen.png|26]] Dark Queen Token – The Elder

## Tiles With Family Effigies
- Gallery – Upper Floor
- Statuary Corridor – Upper Floor

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Ruin the Tribute**
> While on the Ritual Room tile, make a Sanity ![[sanity.png|18]] roll.
> - **4+** You disturb the Altar's arrangement. *The Elder watches on.*
> - **0–3** Take 1 Physical damage. *You have disturbed The Elder.*

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Destroy the Altar**
> While on the tile with the Altar, make a Might ![[might.png|18]] roll. You cannot Destroy the Altar until you have Ruined the Tribute and killed the traitor.
> - **4+** If the traitor is dead, **you win!**
> - **0–3** Take 1 Mental damage. *Arcane punishment for your impudence.*

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Prune the Family Tree**
> While on a tile with Family Effigies, make a Sanity ![[sanity.png|18]] roll.
> - **5+** Remove one Relative from the house. *You chant a spell and one of the relatives turns to dust.*
> - **0–4** Take 1 Physical damage.

## If You Win
*The members of the twisted family age rapidly before your eyes, withering to husks in mere minutes. They collapse and crumble to dust.*
