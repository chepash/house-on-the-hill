**Scenario Card:** None
**Haunt Trigger:** "A Splash of Crimson" · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*As you explored the house, your companions spoke of Crimson Jack, the notorious serial killer who was brought down in this very mansion twenty years ago to the day. They knew that Jack's body had been killed, but they had no idea that his spirit still remained in this old place. You barely manage to suppress your murderous grin as you realize the truth: Jack's back.*

### Setup
1. Your explorer is still in the game. You are the traitor.
2. Place the Monster Card to your left. The monster sometimes takes its turn instead of yours.
3. Heal all of your traits. Gain {1/1/2/2} in both of your Physical traits.

---

## Objective
You win when all of the heroes are dead.

## Tokens Needed
- ![[jacks-spirit.png|26]] Jack's Spirit Token
- ![[sanity.png|26]] 2 Sanity Tokens – Exorcism Circle
- ![[might.png|26]] 2 Might Tokens – Knowledge of Jack

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Stalk the Prey**
> If you haven't attacked yet, and if you do not have line of sight to a hero, you may place your explorer on any Upper or Ground Floor tile that is not in a hero's line of sight.

> [!death] If you die
> Place Jack's Spirit onto the tile with an Omen symbol ![[omen.svg|18]] that is farthest from your corpse. Repeat this process each time you die.

## At the start of your turn, if you are dead, take a turn with Jack's Spirit instead.

## ![[jacks-spirit.png|30]] Monster: Jack's Spirit

> [!monster] Jack's Spirit
> **![[might.png|18]] Might 5 · ![[speed.png|18]] Speed 3 · ![[sanity.png|18]] Sanity 4 · ![[knowledge.png|18]] Knowledge 4**
> - Jack's Spirit may move between adjacent tiles not connected by doorways.
> - Jack's Spirit cannot be stunned.
>
> **At the start of the monster turn**
> If Jack's Spirit is on the same tile as your explorer's corpse, heal all of your traits. You control your explorer again. Remove Jack's Spirit from the house.

## If You Win
*Whistle a jaunty tune. Your friends have all been sliced to bits, and you can once again comfortably take up residence in your old home.*
