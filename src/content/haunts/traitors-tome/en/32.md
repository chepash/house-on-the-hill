**Scenario Card:** Paranormal Investigators
**Haunt Trigger:** Skull · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*The homeowner was eccentric to say the very least, but they were very convincing in their tales of the ghost cat that haunts the house. You follow them as they speak, only to see them transform into an enormous, spectral feline. The creature grins at you, purring, as you are swarmed by an army of undead cats.*

### Setup
1. Cats swarm you. Your explorer has died. Bury your Items and Omens and remove your figure from the house. Place the Bakeneko token on your tile. You now command the Bakeneko and the Undead Cats.
2. Place the Monster Card to your left. The monsters will take their turn in place of yours.
3. Find and set aside {6/5/4/3} Fire tokens ![[fire.png|18]]. Put the rest back in the box.
4. Place {3/4/5/7} Small Monster tokens ![[small-monster.png|18]] in your region. These are your Undead Cats.

---

## Objective
You win when all of the heroes are dead, or when the house has burned down.

## Tokens Needed
- ![[bakeneko.png|26]] Bakeneko Token
- ![[small-monster.png|26]] Small Monster Tokens – Undead Cats
- ![[fire.png|26]] Fire Tokens – Room on Fire

## Instead of your turn, take a turn with the Bakeneko.

## ![[bakeneko.png|30]] Monster: Bakeneko

> [!monster] Bakeneko
> **Might 3\* · Speed 6 · Sanity 6 · Knowledge 2**
> The Bakeneko adds +1 to its Might for each Undead Cat in its region (to a maximum Might of 8).
>
> > [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Burn Them All**
> > While on any tile, choose a tile in line of sight that does not already have a Fire token ![[fire.png|18]] on it. Place a Fire token ![[fire.png|18]] on that tile. When you place the last Fire token ![[fire.png|18]], you win!
>
> **When a hero enters a tile with a Fire token ![[fire.png|18]]**
> They must make a Speed ![[speed.png|18]] roll.
> - **5+** Nothing happens. *They move through the fire without issue.*
> - **0–4** They take 1 Physical damage.
>
> > [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Summon Kitties**
> > While on a tile with an Omen symbol ![[omen.svg|18]], place an Undead Cat on your tile.

## After you take a turn with the Bakeneko, take a turn with each Undead Cat.

## Monster: Undead Cats

> [!monster] Undead Cats
> **Might 3 · Speed 6 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 4**
> Undead Cats are killed instead of stunned when they take damage. They can still be stunned in other ways.
>
> Undead Cats attack with Speed. They deal a maximum of 2 damage per attack.
>
> Undead Cats may not attack any hero that has already taken damage during the Undead Cats' turn. *They like to play with their food.*

## If You Win
*The ghost cat pads through ash and flame, followed by its entourage of undead felines. The troop gathers outside the house, joining in an unearthly chorus of yowls as they watch the Bakeneko's fire reduce the house to rubble.*
