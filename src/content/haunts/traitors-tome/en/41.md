**Scenario Card:** For Sale
**Haunt Trigger:** Idol · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*You feel an electric spark pass through you. Lightning. Thunder. There is a god in the wiring, and the house is its cage. The god commands you to rid the house of intruders. You will listen.*

### Setup
1. Your explorer is still in the game. You are the traitor.
2. Place the Monster Card to your left. The monsters will take their turn after yours.
3. Place a Trap token ![[trap.png|18]] on your tile. Set the remaining Trap tokens ![[trap.png|18]] aside.
4. Take {3/3/4/5} ON/OFF tokens ![[on.png|18]] and return the rest to the box. These are Generators. Place one on your tile and then one on each tile of your choice that has an Item or Omen symbol ![[omen.svg|18]]. If any tokens remain, place these on Landing tiles.

---

## Objective
You win when all of the heroes are dead.

## Tokens Needed
- Number Track
- ![[on.png|26]] 5 ON/OFF tokens – Generators
- ![[trap.png|26]] 5 Trap Tokens
- ![[searched.png|26]] 5 Searched Tokens – Sabotage

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Reactivate Generators**
> While on a tile with a Generator in the OFF position, make a Knowledge ![[knowledge.png|18]] roll.
> - **4+** Flip the Generator to its ON position.
> - **0–3** Nothing Happens.

## Triggering a Trap
When a hero enters a tile with a Trap token ![[trap.png|18]], they take two dice of General damage. The Trap stays on the tile.

## After your turn, take a turn with the House.

## Monster: The House

> [!monster] The House
> **![[might.png|18]] Might 7 · ![[speed.png|18]] Speed 0 · ![[sanity.png|18]] Sanity 5 · ![[knowledge.png|18]] Knowledge 5**
> The House cannot be attacked or stunned. It does not move.
>
> The House may make a Might attack against a hero on or adjacent to any tile with an ON Generator (OFF tokens do not count).
>
> > [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Possess Electronics**
> > Place a Trap on any tile that does not have a hero or Trap on it. You may not use Possess Electronics to place traps on or adjacent to a tile with a Generator.
>
> > [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Twist Hallways**
> > Roll 2 dice. Move any explorer up to that many tiles in any direction. If this causes the explorer to trigger a Trap, they stop moving.

## If You Win
*The house shivers and settles. One day, you will help the thing that lives here to escape this cage. When you do, the world will kneel before the power of this terrible god… and you will be its high priest.*
