**Scenario Card:** A Mysterious Invitation
**Haunt Trigger:** Skull · **Traitor:** Highest Might

## Read First!

### Introduction
*You hoped he wouldn't be awake. After all, you're rather fond of this group, as you were the ones before them. There he was, though, your master—a simple skull, and you are bound to him. He's your host: the Head of the House, and you don't upset him, because you're nothing without him. So instead, your friends are your master's food, fed through the Bloody Room. Through him, they nourish you. He stays content, and you stay alive.*

*So, to the group you say "adieu"—your master is ready to eat.*

### Setup
1. Your explorer is still in the game. You are the traitor.
2. Heal all of your traits. Gain 1 Might.
3. Place the Monster Card to your left. The monsters will take their turn after yours.
4. If the Bloody Room tile has not been discovered, search the tile stack for it and place it on the Ground Floor.
5. Place the Head of the House token on the Bloody Room.
6. Place the Skull under the Monster Card. The Head of the House is carrying it.

---

## Objective
You win when all of the heroes are dead.

## Tokens Needed
- ![[head-of-the-house.png|26]] Head of the House Token

> [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Revive the Host**
> If you are holding the Skull, place the Head of the House on your tile. Then, place the Skull under the Monster Card.

## At the end of each hero's turn
If that hero is carrying the Skull, they make a Speed ![[speed.png|18]] roll.
- **5+** Nothing happens.
- **0–4** The hero takes 2 Physical damage. *Ouch, it bites!*

> [!death] When you die
> Place yourself on the Bloody Room tile and heal all of your traits. *Your service isn't finished.*

## After your turn, take a turn with the Head of the House.

## ![[head-of-the-house.png|30]] Monster: Head of the House

> [!monster] Head of the House
> **Might 6 · Speed 6 · Sanity 6 · Knowledge 8**
> When the Head of the House is defeated by an attack, it is killed instead of stunned. Remove it from the house and give the Skull to the attacker.

## If You Win
*It's a long five minutes as the Bloody Room finishes its digestion. As you stand over your master, handsome clothes gathered for his arrival, flesh crawls from the house's floor and walls, weaving around him. His handsome form gasps to life as you present his suit. He dresses sharply, just like every time before, and he retreats to his bedroom. You're going to have to make some new friends.*
