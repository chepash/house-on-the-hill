**Scenario Card:** Cursed!
**Haunt Trigger:** Armor · **Traitor:** Haunt Revealer

## Read First!

### Introduction
*For days, you and your friends have been having the same nightmare about this mansion, dreams about a blob of flesh that consumes EVERYTHING. You didn't believe it until you saw that old suit of armor. As you leaned in for a closer look, fleshy tendrils burst out of the chest plate, consuming you in an instant.*

### Setup
1. You have been consumed. Bury all of your Items and Omens. Remove your explorer from the house and replace it with the Construct token.
2. Turn the tile you are on face-down. That tile has been Consumed.
3. Place the Monster Card to your left. The monster will take its turn in place of yours.
4. Set the Number Track to {2/3/3/4}. This is your Speed.

---

## Objective
You win when all of the heroes are dead, or when you have Consumed the entire house.

## Tokens Needed
- Number Track
- ![[construct.png|26]] Construct Token
- ![[trap.png|26]] Trap Tokens – Explosives

## Consumed Rooms
Consumed rooms are turned face-down. They are considered to have no symbols or text, and they have doorways on all 4 sides.

## Instead of your turn, take a turn with the Construct.

## ![[construct.png|30]] Monster: The Construct

> [!monster] The Construct
> **![[might.png|18]] Might 8 · ![[speed.png|18]] Speed \* · ![[sanity.png|18]] Sanity 4 · ![[knowledge.png|18]] Knowledge 4**
> The Construct's Speed is equal to the number on the Number Track. (Remember to roll each turn to determine your movement.)
>
> The Construct cannot be stunned.
>
> > [!action] Once during your turn, you may: **Consume Flesh**
> > If you are on a tile with a corpse, you may remove that corpse from the house and bury that explorer's Items and Omens. Move the Number Track up by 1. *You're getting faster.*
>
> > [!action] Multiple times per turn, you may: **Consume the House**
> > While on a non-Landing tile that has not been Consumed, turn that room face-down. That tile has been Consumed. If all non-Landing tiles have been Consumed, **you win!**
> >
> > When you consume a tile with an Omen symbol ![[omen.svg|18]], you may place yourself on any tile with a hero. If you do, lose the rest of your moves for the turn.
> >
> > You may **Consume the House** any number of times per turn.
>
> > [!clarify] Clarification (not from the book)
> > Landing tiles are all three starting tiles: the Basement Landing, the Upper Landing and the whole large ground floor tile (Ground Floor Staircase, Hallway and Entrance Hall). They cannot be Consumed, and you do not need to Consume them to win.

## If You Win
*You release a foul-smelling, gaseous toxin as you finish consuming every living creature in the house. You stretch your fleshy tendrils within your suit of armor, before you shrink back into your home like a hermit crab. It's been a hard day's work.*
