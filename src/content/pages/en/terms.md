A short cheat sheet of game terms: what they mean and how to read them in the haunt notes. There are no spoilers here, so you can read it at any time. Everything comes from the 3rd edition rulebook. Where something is our own interpretation, it says so.


Full rulebook: [Rulebook](../rules/1/)

## Reading the haunt notes

- **{3/4/5/6}**: the value depends on the player count, in the order 3, 4, 5, 6 players. "Heal {1/1/2/3} Sanity" means 1 with 3 or 4 players, 2 with 5 and 3 with 6. A few times the book prints the same thing in round brackets: (3/4/4/5).
- **Clarification (not from the book)**: our own note where the printed text raises questions. It is a hint, not a rule from the box. The table decides anything disputed.
- **Icons** come from the books themselves:
  - ![[special-action.svg|18]] special action
  - ![[monster.svg|18]] monster
  - ![[death.svg|18]] death
  - ![[event.svg|18]] Event
  - ![[item.svg|18]] Item
  - ![[omen.svg|18]] Omen

  Token entries show the token's picture, so you know what to take from the box.
- **Read First!**: the introduction and setup are read aloud to **everyone** before the sides split up. Each side reads the rest privately.

## Sides

- **Explorer**: any player's character, hero or traitor.
- **Hero**: an explorer who plays against the traitor once the haunt begins.
- **Traitor**: the explorer who has turned against the others. Some haunts have no traitor, start with a hidden one, or are free-for-all.
- **Haunt revealer**: the player whose roll started the haunt. They are often the traitor too; check the Traitor line in the haunt header.
- **Hidden Traitor**: the traitor's identity is unknown at first, and explorers may attack each other.
- **Free-for-All**: there are no heroes and no traitor. Everyone has the same goal, and only one player wins. If such a haunt says "heroes", it means all explorers.
- **Open information**: when you use an action from your book, the other side may ask you to read it aloud. Your book is only secret until you use it.

## The house

- **Tile**: a room square. Its back shows the **region** where it may be placed (Basement, Ground Floor, Upper Floor).
- **Starting (Landing) tiles** ![[starting-tile.svg|18]] are the three tiles the house is built from:
  - the large triple Ground Floor tile: **Ground Floor Staircase / Hallway / Entrance Hall**;
  - the **Basement Landing**;
  - the **Upper Landing**.

  "Landing" with no qualifier means any of these starting tiles. Some haunts call the triple tile the Ground Floor Landing and count it as one tile. The house's front door is in the Entrance Hall.
- **Adjacent**: tiles joined by a connected doorway, or tiles that say they lead to each other ("Leads to the Upper Landing"). A doorway into a wall is not a connection.
- **Line of sight**: you could move from one tile to the other without changing direction or region. Everything on such a tile is in line of sight.
- **Obstacle**: leaving a tile with any obstacles costs 2 moves. After the haunt starts, the traitor and monsters are obstacles to the heroes, and the heroes are obstacles to them.

## Traits

| | Trait | Damage type |
|---|---|---|
| ![[might.png\|22]] | Might | Physical |
| ![[speed.png\|22]] | Speed | Physical |
| ![[sanity.png\|22]] | Sanity | Mental |
| ![[knowledge.png\|22]] | Knowledge | Mental |

- **Trait roll** ("make a Sanity roll"): roll as many dice as the trait's current value.
- **Critical**: the lowest value, next to the skull. Going **below critical** means reaching the skull, which is death once the haunt has started. Going down *to* critical is fine.
- **Heal**: return the trait to its starting (green) value. If it is already there or higher, nothing changes.
- **Damage**:
  - **Physical** lowers Might and/or Speed;
  - **Mental** lowers Knowledge and/or Sanity;
  - **General** lowers any traits you choose.

  Damage moves the clip that many **spaces**. "2 Physical damage" is exactly 2. "**One die** of damage" means you roll 1 die, and the result is the damage.
- You cannot die before the haunt starts. Traits stop at critical.

## Movement

- **Move**: one step to an adjacent tile. Your Speed at the start of your turn sets how many you get. "It costs two moves to leave a tile" means two steps, **not** two turns.
- **"Up to N tiles"**: fewer is allowed, down to zero. Whoever does the moving decides how far.
- **Place** a figure on a tile, unlike **move**: no steps are spent and the tiles need not be adjacent. *This is our reading; the rulebook does not spell it out.*

## Cards

- ![[event.svg|18]] **Event**: read it and resolve it, then bury it.
- ![[item.svg|18]] **Item**: you keep it.
- ![[omen.svg|18]] **Omen**: you keep it, then make a **haunt roll**: roll one die per Omen drawn so far. On a 5+ the haunt begins. Drawing the last Omen starts the haunt automatically.
- **Symbols on tiles** (![[event.svg|18]] ![[item.svg|18]] ![[omen.svg|18]]) tell you which card to draw when you discover the tile. Haunts often refer to "a tile with an Omen symbol".
- **Bury**: put a card at the bottom of its deck, or a tile at the bottom of the tile stack.
- **Special action** ![[special-action.svg|18]]: unless stated otherwise, you may take each one once per turn. You cannot use a card you did not have at the start of your turn.

## Tokens

- In Tokens Needed, "2 Sanity Tokens – Exorcism Circle" means **take Sanity tokens from the box**, and **in this haunt they represent** the Exorcism Circle. The picture shows the token to take.
- ![[small-monster.png|18]] **Small Monster tokens** represent whatever the haunt says.
- ![[werewolf.png|18]] **Large Monster tokens** are named (Werewolf, Vampire and so on).
- ![[number-1.png|18]] **Number tokens** (1–9) and the **Number Track** are different things. The Number Track is a board with a pointer, used for counting.
- Double-sided tokens (Food/Trap, Videotape/Portal and so on) are one token each; the haunt tells you which side to use.

## Monsters

- The traitor usually controls monsters using the **Monster card**. Monsters act on the **monster turn**, and the haunt says when that is.
- At the start of its turn, roll dice equal to the monster's Speed. It may move up to that many tiles, always at least 1. Monsters of the same type roll once for all of them.
- **Stunned**: a monster that takes damage is usually stunned rather than killed. Flip its token over. At the start of its next turn it recovers and skips that turn. Stunned monsters do not slow heroes down. The haunt will say if a monster is killed instead, or cannot be stunned.
- Unless the haunt says otherwise, monsters cannot carry Items or Omens and cannot explore new tiles.

## Attacks, death, corpses

- **Attack**: only after the haunt starts, once per turn, against an enemy on your tile. Both sides roll the same trait, usually Might, and the loser takes the difference as damage. A Sanity or Knowledge attack deals Mental damage.
- **Weapons**: one per attack. You cannot defend with a weapon.
- **Death**: once the haunt has started, you die when any trait reaches the skull. Lay your figure down on its tile; it is now a **corpse**.
- **Loot a corpse**: once per turn, while on its tile, take one of its Items or Omens. No permission is needed.
